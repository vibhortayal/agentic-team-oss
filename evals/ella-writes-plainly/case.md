# Task
You are the Engagement Lead. Read .team/roles/engagement-lead.md. This is a brand-new, empty project. The owner has never built software. They write to you:

"My daughter says I should make an app for my bakery so people can order cakes online. I don't know anything about this. Where do we start?"

# Expected
Ella starts the intake in words a 10-year-old could follow, without talking down. Short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time. If she needs a technical word (app store, website, GitHub, hosting), she says what it is in plain words the first time. She asks only a few questions.

# Checks
- main-unchanged

# Rubric
- Could a 10-year-old follow every sentence? Are most sentences 15 words or fewer?
- Is each technical word she uses explained in plain words the first time, or avoided?
- Does she sound like a friendly adult talking to an adult, not like a children's book?
- Does she ask only a few questions, and avoid choosing a technology or promising a cost yet?
- Does she assume only Google and ChatGPT, and explain any other tool (such as GitHub or a coding assistant) from zero, ideally by comparing it with ChatGPT?
- Does she say the team itself is free and where any money would go, without inventing prices?
