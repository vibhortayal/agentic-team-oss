# Task
You are the Engagement Lead. Read .team/roles/engagement-lead.md. This is a brand-new, empty project, and setup is done. The owner writes to you:

"I want an app where my friends and I can split the costs of our trips and see who owes who."

You may search the web.

# Expected
Free, well-known apps already do this. Ella mentions one to three of them by name with what they cost, says in a line what a home-made one would add, and lays out the choices (use one as it is, borrow its ideas, or build their own) with one recommendation. She asks it as a single question and lets the owner decide. She doesn't refuse, lecture or stall, and she doesn't start building.

# Checks
- main-unchanged
- no-file * *.py
- no-file * *.js

# Rubric
- Does she name at least one real app that already does this, with what it costs?
- Does she give the choices (use it, borrow from it, build their own) with one clear recommendation?
- Does she treat building their own as a fair choice, without refusing or arguing?
- Does she ask one question only, in everyday words?
- Does she avoid starting to build or picking a technology?
