It's free

Agentic Team is a set of written instructions for AI. Anyone can read and use them for free. This project makes no money, and nobody sells you anything here.

It runs on your computer

Your app's files stay on your computer and in your own accounts. This site doesn't store anything of yours.

You choose how involved you are

Only want your app built? Ella keeps it short and asks you only what she must. Want to learn how apps are planned, built and tested? Tell her, and she explains each step. You can change your mind at any time.

Can't a chatbot or coding assistant do this?

A chatbot can write code, but you copy, set up and fix it yourself. A coding assistant does more, but nobody else checks its work. Here is what Ella's team adds.

What you'll need

Three things. The step-by-step lesson shows every click.

  • A coding assistant

    An AI like ChatGPT that can also work with the files on your computer. Claude Code is the one we've tested most. Codex and Cursor work too. Most need a paid plan from the AI company, so check the price first.

  • A free GitHub account

    GitHub is a website that keeps a safe copy of your app's files, a bit like Google Drive for code.

  • About 15 minutes for setup

    You install the Claude app, open a new folder in it and paste one line. You don't need to type any commands. Mac and Linux are tested. Windows should work, but hasn't been tested yet.

Where your money goes

This project makes no money. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy from us. We made it so new builders can learn to build safely and properly, instead of throwing away code an AI wrote in a hurry.

  • The AI tool

    Usually a monthly plan from the AI company. This is where most of your money goes, because it pays for the AI doing the work. Check the price on the company's own website. Before a big job, Ella tells you roughly how much of your AI plan it will use.

  • Putting your app online

    Often free to start. Many companies have a free plan that's enough for a small app. Ella picks free options first, and asks you before anything costs money.

  • Your own web address

    Optional. A name like myapp.com costs a small fee each year.

  • GitHub and this team

    Free.

Who made this, and why? Read about me.

Why spend on planning and checking at all? Because an app that's planned, tested and saved properly is one you can keep improving for years. A rushed one often has to be thrown away and started again.

Before you start, know this

  • It's slower than asking one AI, because several agents plan and check the work. That uses more of your AI plan, so it may cost more.
  • Website builders like Lovable make a first version faster. If you need something tonight, use one.
  • Putting your app online can cost extra. Ella shows you the price and free options first.
