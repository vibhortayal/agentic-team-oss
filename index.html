How it works

Ella is an AI you chat with. Behind her is a team of AI helpers, called agents. Each one has one job.

  1. Tell Ella your idea

    Use your own words. She asks a few questions until she understands it.

  2. Get a one-page plan

    It says what will be built and what it will cost. Nothing is spent without your yes.

  3. The team builds and checks it

    One AI builds. A different AI checks the work, like a teacher marking homework.

  4. Try it, then say go

    You try your app first. It only goes online when you're happy with it.

Can't ChatGPT do this?

What you'll need

Three things. The step-by-step lesson shows every click.

  • A coding assistant

    An AI like ChatGPT that can also work with the files on your computer. Claude Code is the one we've tested most. Codex and Cursor work too. Most need a paid plan from the AI company, so check the price first.

  • A free GitHub account

    GitHub is a website that keeps a safe copy of your app's files, a bit like Google Drive for code.

  • About 15 minutes for setup

    You install the Claude app, open a new folder in it and paste one line. You don't need to type any commands.

Where your money goes

This project makes no money. There are no ads, no sign-up and nothing to buy from us. We made it so new builders can learn to build safely and properly, instead of throwing away code an AI wrote in a hurry.

  • The AI tool

    Usually a monthly plan from the AI company. This is where most of your money goes, because it pays for the AI doing the work. Check the price on the company's own website. Ella tells you before a long job uses a lot.

  • Putting your app online

    Often free to start. Many companies have a free plan that's enough for a small app. Ella picks free options first, and asks you before anything costs money.

  • Your own web address

    Optional. A name like myapp.com costs a small fee each year.

  • GitHub and this team

    Free.

Why spend on planning and checking at all? Because an app that's planned, tested and saved properly is one you can keep improving for years. A rushed one often has to be thrown away and started again.

Before you start, know this

  • It's new. It hasn't built a real app all the way from idea to launch yet.
  • It's slower than asking one AI, and it uses more AI, so your plan may cost more.
  • Website builders like Lovable make a first version faster. If you need something tonight, use one.
  • Putting your app online can cost extra. Ella shows you the price and free options first.
