Your first project

From zero to your first conversation with Ella. You get things ready once. After that, each new app starts at step 2. No terminal needed. If you get stuck, tell Claude what you see. It will walk you through it.

1

Get four things ready

You only do this once. You won't need the terminal, the plain window where people type commands.

The Claude appThe Claude app for Mac or Windows. It's like ChatGPT, but its Code tab can also work with the files on your computer. That needs a paid plan: Pro is about $20 a month. Why not ChatGPT?
Download it →
GitHub accountA free website that keeps a safe copy of your app's files, a bit like Google Drive for code.
Create one →
GitA free program that keeps a history of your app's files. Install it like any other app. Most Macs already have it. If yours doesn't, Claude tells you during setup.
Install Git →
Password managerAn app that keeps your passwords safe. Bitwarden is free. Your project's passwords and keys go there, never in a chat.
Get Bitwarden →
2

Open a new folder in the Code tab

Open the Claude app and sign in. Then:

  1. Click Code at the top of the side bar.
  2. Under the chat box, click the first button and pick Local. That means "work on this computer".
  3. Click the folder button next to it, then Open folder…. Make a new folder for your app, such as my-grocery-list, and pick it.
  4. Leave the mode on Manual. Then Claude asks you before it changes a file or runs anything.
it looks like a normal chat, just with your folder attached
3

Paste one line

Paste this into the chat, with your own idea at the end, and press Enter. Claude copies the team to your computer, adds it to your folder and saves a first snapshot. If something is missing, like Git, it tells you what to do. Each time it asks to do something, it says what and why. Click Allow if that sounds right.

Set up Agentic Team here by following https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{ site.github.repository_nwo }}/main/START.md. My idea: I want an app where my family can share a grocery list.
swap in your own idea, in your own words
4

Meet Ella

When setup is done, Ella says hello and asks about your idea. Next time, open the same folder in the Code tab and paste this one line. It's the only prompt you need to remember.

You are Ella, the Engagement Lead. Read AGENTS.md and .team/roles/engagement-lead.md. I'd like to add a shopping budget.
5

Answer her questions

Ella interviews you, a few questions at a time. Something like:

Ella: Who will use it: just your family, or anyone? And should it work on phones?
You: Just the four of us, on our phones.
Ella: Got it. Then I'd keep it simple: one shared list, sign-in with Google, free hosting. I'll put the plan and the costs on one page for you.
6

Read the plan and say yes

You'll get one page. It says what we'll build and what it costs each month. It lists the few steps that are yours, who's on the team, and when Ella will need you. Ask "tell me more" about anything. When you say yes, the team gets to work. Ella comes back when you're needed: to try a preview, to OK any spending, and to say go for the launch.

that's it, you've started
