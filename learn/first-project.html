Lesson 04 · 8 minutes

Your first project

From zero to your first conversation with Ella. You'll do the setup once; after that, every project starts at step 4. If you get stuck anywhere, skip ahead to step 4 and tell Ella you're starting from scratch. She'll walk you through the rest.

1

Get four things ready

Each is free to start. You only do this once.

GitHub accountWhere your project and its whole history live, in your name.
Create one →
An AI coding toolClaude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI or similar. The agents run inside it.
Which ones work →
Git and a terminalThe terminal is the text window where you paste commands.
Install Git →
Password managerBitwarden is free. Your project's keys go there, never in a chat.
Get Bitwarden →
2

Download the team

Open your terminal and paste this. It copies the team to your computer, once.

git clone {{ site.github.repository_url }} ~/agentic-team
↑ one time only, for all your projects
3

Put the team in your project

Pick a folder for your project and run the installer for your tool. It copies the team in and leaves a note telling your AI tool how to find them.

# any tool ~/agentic-team/adapters/generic/install.sh ~/code/my-project # or, if you use Claude Code ~/agentic-team/adapters/claude-code/install.sh ~/code/my-project

Then save (commit) the new files in your project. The full guide has the optional extras.

4

Say hello to Ella

Open your AI tool in the project folder and paste this one line, with your idea at the end. It's the only prompt you need to remember.

You are Ella, the Engagement Lead. Read AGENTS.md and .team/roles/engagement-lead.md. I want an app where my family can share a grocery list.
swap in your own idea, in your own words
5

Answer her questions

Ella interviews you, a few questions at a time. Something like:

Ella: Who will use it: just your family, or anyone? And should it work on phones?
You: Just the four of us, on our phones.
Ella: Got it. Then I'd keep it simple: one shared list, sign-in with Google, free hosting. I'll put the plan and the costs on one page for you.
6

Read the plan and say yes

You'll get one page: what we'll build, what you'll need and what it costs each month, the few steps that are yours, who's on the team, and when she'll need you. Ask "tell me more" about anything. When you approve, the team gets to work, and Ella comes back when you're needed: to try a preview, to okay any spending, and to say go for the launch.

that's it, you've started
