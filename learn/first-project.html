Your first project

From zero to your first conversation with Ella. You get things ready once. After that, each new app starts at step 2. No terminal needed. If you get stuck, tell Claude what you see. It will walk you through it.

1

Get two things ready

You only do this once. Ella installs everything else for you during setup, like Git, a program that keeps a history of your files. She asks first, and you click Yes when your computer asks.

Mac and Linux are tested. Windows should work, but nobody has tested it all the way yet.

The Claude appAn AI like ChatGPT that can also work with the files on your computer. Install it and sign in. It usually needs a paid plan, so check the price first. Other coding assistants work too. Why not ChatGPT?
Get the Claude app → · Others that work →
A safe place for keysYour app will need a few keys. A key is a long password that lets your app use another service. Keep them in a password manager (your phone or computer may already have one built in), or in a private file outside your project folder. Never paste a key into a chat.

No GitHub account yet? That's fine. GitHub is a free website that keeps a safe copy of your app's files, a bit like Google Drive for code. During setup, Ella opens the sign-up page and helps you through it. You type the details yourself, because GitHub checks that you're a person.

2

Open a new folder in the Claude app

In the Claude app, click Code in the side bar. Pick Local, which means "work on this computer". Then click Open folder… and make a new folder for your app, such as my-grocery-list. Leave the mode on Manual, so Claude asks before it changes a file or runs anything. Each time, it says what and why. Click Allow if that sounds right.

Using a terminal instead? Open it in the folder and type claude (or codex). With Cursor, open the app and pick the folder.

3

Paste one line

Paste this into the chat, with your own idea at the end. Ella says hello and gets your computer ready. She asks before she installs anything. You'll click Yes a few times, make a GitHub account if you need one, and click Authorize once in your browser. Then she adds the team to your folder and makes your app's private home on GitHub. The first time takes about 15 minutes. After that, a new app takes a minute.

Set up Agentic Team here by following https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{ site.github.repository_nwo }}/main/START.md. My idea: I want an app where my family can share a grocery list.
swap in your own idea, in your own words

Using Claude Code? You can use our plugin instead. Type /plugin install agentic-team --marketplace {{ site.github.repository_nwo }}, then /agentic-team:start and your idea.

4

Meet Ella

When setup is done, Ella asks about your idea. Next time, open the same folder in the Claude app and paste this one line. It's the only prompt you need to remember.

You are Ella, the Engagement Lead. Read AGENTS.md and .team/roles/engagement-lead.md. I'd like to add a shopping budget.
5

Answer her questions

Ella interviews you, a few questions at a time. Something like:

Ella: Who will use it: just your family, or anyone? And should it work on phones?
You: Just the four of us, on our phones.
Ella: Got it. Then I'd keep it simple: one shared list, sign-in with Google, free hosting. I'll put the plan and the costs on one page for you.
6

Read the plan and say yes

You'll get one page. It says what we'll build and what it costs each month. It lists the few steps that are yours, who's on the team, and when Ella will need you. Ask "tell me more" about anything. When you say yes, the team gets to work. Ella comes back when you're needed: to try a preview and say yes or what to change, to OK any spending, and to say go for the launch. You never press merge on GitHub; Ella does it after your yes.

that's it, you've started
