The rules we follow

Every agent on the team follows the same short list of rules. A role can add rules of its own, but never weaken one of these. Here they are in plain words.

You're in charge

01

You decide what can't be undone.

Launching, spending money, publishing anything, and anything touching passwords or keys wait for your own yes. An agent saying "approved" doesn't count.

02

You only talk to Ella.

Any agent that needs you goes through her, with exactly what it needs and what it suggests.

03

One recommendation, with reasons.

Every decision comes with pros, cons and one clear recommendation. If you disagree, the advice changes only when you give a new fact, not just because you asked again.

04

A price before anything is spent.

Every paid service comes with its monthly price and a question. Free options come first. Before a long job, you get a rough cost.

Someone else checks the work

05

The builder never accepts their own work.

A separate agent checks it, every time.

06

Checks come from the plan, before the code.

Vera writes what she'll test from what you agreed, before she sees what Ian built.

07

Agents can't change the tests.

Automatic checks stop the builder from editing existing tests.

Results come with proof

08

Every claim comes with proof.

The exact command and its real output are saved in the project, so anyone can check them. You never need to read it yourself. It's there so another agent, or a helper you trust, can check.

09

Say what wasn't checked.

If something wasn't checked, the agent says so. If it runs out of time, it tells you what's stuck.

10

Three failed checks, then stop.

After three failed checks on the same piece, the team stops and tells you in plain words what went wrong.

Everything is written down

11

The project keeps the memory.

Plans, choices and the notes agents pass to each other are kept in your project on GitHub. So any agent, in any tool, can pick up the work.

12

Write little, for people.

Short and plain. Each fact is written in one place only, so two pages never disagree. A project nobody can read is a project nobody can check.

This is the plain-words version. The full list, with every detail the agents follow, is in Company principles.
