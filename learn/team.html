Meet the team

Ten agents, one job each. You only talk to Ella; she brings in the others when your project needs them. Tap a card to see what they'd say to you.

Who remembers what. Ella, Priya, Arjun, Bea and Otto keep notes in your project's repository, so they pick up where they left off. Ian, Vera, Rohan, Quinn and Max start each task fresh, so earlier work doesn't sway their judgement.
