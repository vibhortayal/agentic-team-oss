Meet the team

Ten agents, one job each. You only talk to Ella. She brings in only the ones your project needs. Tap a face to jump to that agent, or tap a card to see what they'd say to you.

Why ten? Do I need them all?

No. Ten is every job a small software company has, from the first idea to keeping the app running: planning, building, checking, watching the money and more. Most projects need only a few of them.

Ella picks the smallest team that fits your idea, and tells you who she picked and why. A small test app might need only three or four agents. Ella is the only one who is always there. You can ask her to add someone or leave someone out.

Can I rename them? Yes, any of them, Ella too. Just tell Ella the new name, and the whole team will use it from then on.

Who remembers what. Ella, Priya, Arjun, Bea and Otto keep notes in your project's repository, so they pick up where they left off. Ian, Vera, Rohan, Quinn and Max start each task fresh, so they look at each piece with fresh eyes.
