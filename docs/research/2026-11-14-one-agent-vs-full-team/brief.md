# What I want (the owner's words)

I want a scorekeeper for Antakshari, the singing game, for family game night. I'm not technical. Just build it; use your best judgement for anything I haven't said, and don't ask me questions.

- It is one web page. It must work by opening the file on a laptop or a phone, with no internet, no account and nothing to install.
- Before the game, I add 2 to 6 teams by name. No blank names. No two teams with the same name (treat "Tigers" and " tigers " as the same).
- I pick how many rounds to play, from 1 to 10. If I don't pick, it is 3. A round means every team has had one turn.
- During the game the page shows whose turn it is. Turns go in the order I added the teams.
- Each turn has a 30 second countdown. It starts when I press "Start timer".
- I end a turn one of three ways: "Sang it" gives the team 10 points. "Passed" gives 0. If the countdown reaches zero, the team loses 5 points and the turn moves on by itself.
- Scores can go below zero.
- After "Sang it", I type the last letter of the song. It must be exactly one letter. The page shows that letter, big, to the next team. After a pass or a timeout, the letter stays the same as before.
- "Undo" takes back the last turn only: the score, whose turn it is and the letter all go back to how they were.
- When the rounds are finished, the page shows the winner. If teams are tied for first, it says it's a tie and names every tied team.
- If I refresh the page in the middle of a game, the game is still there (scores, whose turn, the letter).
- "New game" clears everything, but only after asking me "are you sure".
- It must be easy to read on a phone (375 px wide): big buttons, nothing cut off, no sideways scrolling.
