    Scores

      Final scores

        1. Add 2 to 6 teams. Pick how many rounds. Then press "Start game".
        2. The page shows whose turn it is. Press "Start timer" when the team is ready. They have 30 seconds.
        3. If they sing a song, press "Sang it". Then type the last letter of their song. They get 10 points.
        4. If they give up, press "Passed". They get 0 points.
        5. If the time runs out, they lose 5 points. The page moves to the next team by itself. "Sang it" and "Passed" switch off for one second after that, so a late press can't land on the wrong team.
        6. The next team starts their song with the big letter.
        7. Made a mistake? Press "Undo". It takes back the last turn, and only that one.
        8. Your game is kept if you refresh the page. "New game" clears it.
        9. If the laptop sleeps while the timer runs, the time still counts. If a team loses points that way, press "Undo".
        10. This page is made for a laptop. It may work on a phone. Try it on your phone before game night. If the buttons do nothing there, use the laptop.
