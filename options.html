Other options

This isn't the only project like this, and several of these are bigger and more tested. Look at them too, and pick what fits you. Four things to check: who it's for, what it costs, where your app's files live, and who checks the work.

Free and open source. Anyone can read them, and you run them in a coding assistant.

  • BMAD MethodA team of AI agents, such as an analyst, a product manager and an architect, that plan and build with you. The closest to this project.
  • Spec KitGitHub's own toolkit for writing a clear plan first, then having the AI build from it.
  • OpenSpecA lighter way to agree a plan with the AI before it changes your app.
  • SuperpowersA very popular set of skills that teach a coding assistant to plan, test and review its own work.
  • MetaGPT and ChatDevResearch projects that run a pretend software company made of AI agents. Good to learn from.
  • OpenHandsAn open-source AI developer that writes and runs code for you.

Paid products. Most have a free plan to try.

  • Lovable, Bolt and ReplitYou build an app by chatting, on their website. The fastest way to a first version.
  • AtomsA team of "AI employees" that builds apps and websites with no code.
  • KiroA coding tool from Amazon Web Services where AI agents plan, build and check the work.
  • Devin and FactoryAI coding agents made for engineering teams at companies.

Last checked November 2026. Prices and plans change, so check each site.
